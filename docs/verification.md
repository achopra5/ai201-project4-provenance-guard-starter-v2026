## Outcome

signal and independent surface stylometry, an equal-weight score, the three
approved labels, and persistent `POST /appeal` review requests. Both signal
values remain in responses and decision logs for Unit 8. Invalid input is
rejected before inference where possible. Original decisions remain in the log.

The owner approved the five criteria, 0.5/0.5 weights, inclusive human boundary
0.20, inclusive AI boundary 0.85, and exact reader labels in this chat. The
unsure interval is strictly between the boundaries. These choices prioritize
avoiding false accusations and accept missed AI writing. No automatic penalty
or extra disagreement adjustment is applied. Scores are heuristic indices.

## Limits and next-unit work

Neither predictability nor stylometry proves authorship; both are biased,
length/domain dependent, and easy to move without changing an author's identity.
The model only sees the first 400 tokens; style sees the whole accepted input.
Known historical human text may be memorized by GPT-2. The style fallback is 0.5
below 20 words or two nonempty sentences; those submissions can only be unsure.
There is no calibrated error-rate guarantee, and the sample false-positive
criterion is not an estimate of the population rate.

This local service has no caller authentication or actual staffed review queue.
The append-only file persists status but is not a multi-worker database; its
lock coordinates threads only. Raw writing is not retained in decision logs,
so a production platform would need to retain submissions separately for review.
The appeal ID associates the request with a submission but does not authenticate
its writer. Unit 8 will add rejection telemetry/rate limits and run its attacks
and repeated criterion evaluation with these limitations visible.
